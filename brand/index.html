Mazal — brand

A tagine whose knob is a fresh leaf: Moroccan food, still good. Majorelle blue for the brand and every primary action in the app; green only for freshness and savings.

Logo

Primary — on white and light backgrounds
Reversed — on Majorelle blue (leaf in mint)

Symbol and app icon

Symbol — avatars, favicons, small spaces
App icon — white symbol on Majorelle
Symbol reversed — dark backgrounds

Colors

Majorelle #6050DCBrand, primary buttons, selected states, links, active tab. White text 5.71:1.
Majorelle pressed #4A3BC2Pressed primary button. White text 7.79:1.
Ink #111111Text and icons. 18.88:1 on white.
Green #0B7A4BLeaf on white, savings, "Open now", success. 5.39:1 on white.
Mint #9FE3BFLeaf on Majorelle only (3.86:1, graphic use).

Sizes

Minimum: logo 96 px wide, symbol 24 px.

Rules

  • Clear space around the logo: at least the height of the leaf on every side.
  • The leaf is green on light backgrounds and mint on Majorelle; never blue, never green on blue.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or change the blue.
  • The wordmark is drawn, not typed: use the files in logo/, not a font. App text uses Inter.
